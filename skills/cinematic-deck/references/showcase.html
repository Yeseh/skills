Authoring

It's just HTML.

Press → to walk through the markup, one attribute at a time.

A slide is a <section>. Its attributes choose the hue and how it enters.

data-split breaks a heading into letters or words that enter in sequence.

data-model writes an input into state. data-tpl reads it back anywhere.

<deck-edge> connects two diagram nodes; flow animates traffic on it.

data-step turns any element into a build, like this text.

<section class="slide" data-hue="200" data-transition="zoom"> <h2 data-split>Hello, world</h2> <input data-model="name"> <p data-tpl="Hi, {name|friend}"></p> <deck-edge from="lb" to="api" flow="dash"></deck-edge> <p data-step="1">Next press</p> </section>

Five presses. Lines highlight with data-focus; the annotation swaps with data-step plus data-step-until.
